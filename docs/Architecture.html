v6

earlier2026-11-18 · job pool, fairness, model limits
ChangeWhatWhy
decidedOne shared pool for all projects: the JOB table holds all jobs of all projects; the Redis streams only runnable ones. Workers take whatever comes next; results go to the stream of the orchestrator holding the project; a late result for a dead orchestrator is lost and the job runs againAndreas, 2026-11-18: workers work where most is needed
decidedCap per project: at most N jobs of a project in the pool at once (queued plus running), the engine holds back the restAndreas, 2026-11-18. A 5,000-page Akt must not crowd out a small project
decidedPriority stream for jobs from "Apply changes", checked first by workersAndreas, 2026-11-18. Quick feedback on editor corrections
decidedSemaphore per model: an AI job takes a slot before its model call; without a free slot it goes back to the pool with a growing delay; slots are leases with expiry; going back is no attempt and no timeout raise; rate-limit answers (HTTP 429) handled the same wayAndreas, 2026-11-18 (his proposal). Respects provider limits per model; avoids busy loops and slots lost to dead workers
openLimits on tokens per minute: not covered; a token bucket on the same principle if neededThe semaphore limits only concurrent calls
droppedSeparate streams per model or providerReplaced by the semaphore per model
