Job Graph

How the scheduling core (engine/graph.py on branch engine-robustness, not merged yet) is linked: where it sits, what one job goes through, how jobs block each other, and which job types exist.

1. Where the graph sits

The graph is pure logic: no database, no Redis. The engine (one per project) is the shell around it. It always writes a decision to PostgreSQL first and only then tells the graph, so the graph only ever holds committed state.

2. What one job goes through

Status in the database: waiting, queued, done, failed. "Running" exists only in Redis. Done and failed jobs leave the graph; they stay in the database.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> waiting: created
  waiting --> queued: runnable (dependencies done, targets free) → sent, attempt + 1
  queued --> done: result OK
  queued --> waiting: 1st failure → timeout × 2
  queued --> waiting: lease expired → same timeout
  queued --> failed: 2nd failure
  queued --> failed: attempts reached max_attempts
  queued --> failed: result cannot be written (no retry)
  waiting --> failed: a job it waits on failed
  done --> [*]
  failed --> [*]

New in T-35: the attempt cap (max_attempts in the config), "result cannot be written" fails one job instead of stopping the project, and a job waiting on a failed job fails too instead of waiting forever.

3. How jobs block each other

Two kinds of links decide when a waiting job may run:

  • Dependencies (table JOB_DEPENDENCY): "B may only start after A is done". Not used by any job type yet; decide_border (T-21) will be the first, waiting for the reading of every page in its window.
  • Targets (table JOB_TARGET): each job reads or writes objects (a file, a page). A job only starts if none of its write targets is in use, and none of its read targets is being written.

Graph operations at a glance

OperationWhat it does
addAdopts new or loaded jobs with their open dependencies and targets
runnable(limit)Waiting jobs with all dependencies done and targets free, by priority then age, at most the free slots
sentMarks jobs as in the pool under a new attempt
decideTurns a result (done, failed, lease expired) into transitions; ignores older attempts
failLasting failure without retry, e.g. a result that cannot be written
commitApplies transitions after the database is written; done jobs free their dependents
dropForgets deleted jobs (stage reset)
is_idleNothing in the pool and nothing that could run: the engine may be unloaded
